Moving funds into and out of Perpetra is straightforward, but there are a few mechanics worth understanding before you start — particularly the per-token withdrawal cooldown that activates on every deposit. This page walks you through both flows and explains what happens to your balance along the way.

## What You Can Deposit

Perpetra accepts **HBAR** directly, along with any token the protocol has explicitly approved. Only approved tokens can be deposited — this protects you from accidentally sending in an unsupported asset that the platform cannot credit or return cleanly.

<Note>
  Not every deposit-eligible token can be used as trading collateral. A token can be accepted into the platform without being approved to margin a position. Check the app to confirm which tokens are both depositable and collateral-eligible before funding your account.
</Note>

## How to Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Open Perpetra and connect with **HashPack** (recommended) or any **WalletConnect**-compatible wallet. Make sure you are on the Hedera network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a token and amount">
    Choose the asset you want to deposit — HBAR or an approved HTS token — and enter the amount. The interface shows your wallet balance alongside the platform's supported token list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deposit transaction">
    Sign and submit the transaction from your wallet. Perpetra credits your account based on the amount **actually received** — if a token applies a transfer fee or cut, you are credited the net amount that arrives, not the gross amount you sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your balance">
    Once the transaction is confirmed on-chain, your Perpetra balance updates and the funds are ready to use for trading. If a points or rewards program is active, your deposit counts toward it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Every deposit starts — or **restarts** — a withdrawal cooldown timer for that specific token. See the section below for details.
</Note>

## The Withdrawal Cooldown

Each time you deposit a given token, a cooldown timer starts for that token. You cannot withdraw that token until the cooldown has passed.

A few important specifics:

- The cooldown is **per-token**, not per-account. Depositing HBAR does not affect the cooldown on any other token you hold.
- The timer **resets on every deposit** of that token, not just your first. If you deposit the same token a second time before the first cooldown expires, the clock restarts from the moment of the new deposit.

<Note>
  If you plan to withdraw soon, avoid making an additional deposit of the same token in the meantime — doing so will reset your cooldown and delay your withdrawal by the full waiting period again.
</Note>

## How Withdrawals Are Processed

When you submit a withdrawal request, the platform checks three conditions before releasing your funds:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cooldown check">
    Has the withdrawal waiting period for that token passed since your last deposit? If not, the withdrawal is blocked until it has.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Balance check">
    Does your account hold at least the amount you are requesting? Your withdrawal cannot exceed your available balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Platform liquidity check">
    Does the platform have enough of that token freely available right now? Even if your balance is sufficient, some tokens may be temporarily less liquid — for example, if collateral is tied up backing open positions elsewhere in the system. This safety check ensures withdrawals are only ever paid from funds that are genuinely free.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If all three checks pass, the funds are sent to your wallet immediately.

## Checking Your Balance

Your current balance for any token — along with whether that token is supported and whether it is approved as collateral — is always available to query in the app. This lets you confirm your available balance and collateral eligibility before placing trades or requesting a withdrawal.

## Fees and Balance Movements

Not every balance change in your account represents a deposit or withdrawal of tokens. Trading fees are deducted from your account balance and routed to the Insurance Fund and Treasury as bookkeeping entries — no token actually leaves the platform until those fees are periodically distributed. Similarly, liquidation fees are credited to the keeper who performed the liquidation rather than flowing out as a withdrawal.

<Tip>
  Think of your Perpetra balance as an account ledger. Deposits and withdrawals move tokens across the platform boundary; everything else — fees, funding payments, P&L — is an entry that updates your ledger without a corresponding on-chain transfer at that moment.
</Tip>
