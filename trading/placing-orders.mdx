Every trade on Perpetra starts from the order panel on the trading page. Before you begin, make sure you have deposited USDC collateral into your account — the order panel will prompt you to do this if you haven't yet. Once your collateral is in place, follow the steps below to open your first position.

<Note>
  If you haven't added collateral yet, the submit button shows **Deposit to Trade** instead of **Open Long** or **Open Short**. Complete a deposit first before attempting to place an order. See [Deposits & Withdrawals](/trading/deposits-and-withdrawals) for instructions.
</Note>

## Opening a position

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a market">
    Select the market you want to trade from the market selector at the top of
    the page. Perpetra offers 13 markets quoted in USDC, including HBAR-USDC,
    BTC-USDC, ETH-USDC, and 10 additional Hedera Token Service (HTS) markets.
    The header bar above the chart shows the market's mark price, index price,
    24-hour volume, open interest, current funding rate, time until the next
    funding tick, and the index price source (Supra, Chainlink, or SaucerSwap
    TWAP, depending on the market).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a side">
    Select **Long** or **Short** at the top of the order panel. Go long if you
    expect the price to rise; go short if you expect it to fall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an order type">
    Pick **Market** or **Limit** from the order type tabs.

    - **Market** fills immediately against the best available price on the book.
      Use this when speed of execution matters more than the exact fill price.
    - **Limit** rests on the book at your chosen price until a counterparty
      fills it or you cancel it. Use this when you want price certainty.

    The **TP/SL** tab is visible but not yet functional. See [Order Types &
    Reduce-Only](/trading/order-types-and-reduce-only) for the full breakdown of
    each type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your size">
    Enter your position size in USDC. Use the **25% / 50% / 75% / Max**
    quick-select buttons to size your trade as a percentage of your available
    balance. The order info section below the input updates in real time as you
    type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your leverage">
    Drag the leverage slider to set your exposure multiplier, anywhere from
    **1× to 50×**. Higher leverage amplifies both gains and losses and moves
    your liquidation price closer to your entry. The order info section shows
    you the estimated liquidation price before you confirm.

    See [Positions & Margin](/trading/positions-and-margin) for how leverage
    interacts with your collateral and margin requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your margin mode">
    Select **Cross** or **Isolated** margin from the toggle in the order panel.

    - **Cross** pools your collateral across all cross-margin positions,
      allowing profitable positions to support positions under pressure.
    - **Isolated** walls off a fixed amount of collateral for this position
      only, so a liquidation here cannot affect the rest of your account.

    See [Margin Modes](/trading/margin-modes) for a full comparison and
    guidance on when to use each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and sign">
    Before submitting, review the **Order Info** section at the bottom of the
    panel. It shows your position size, estimated entry price, estimated
    liquidation price, and the taker fee for a market order (or maker fee for a
    limit order).

    When you're ready, click **Open Long** or **Open Short**. Your wallet
    prompts you to sign an **EIP-712 message** — this is gasless and costs
    nothing at signing time. The signed order is then sent to Perpetra's
    matching engine for execution and on-chain settlement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after you sign

Your signed order is forwarded to Perpetra's off-chain matching engine. What happens next depends on the order type:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Market order" icon="bolt">
    Your order matches immediately against the best available resting orders on
    the opposite side. Once the fill is settled on-chain, you'll see a **toast
    notification** confirming the trade and the position will appear in the
    **Positions** panel below the chart.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Limit order" icon="clock">
    If your price isn't immediately available in the book, the order rests until
    a counterparty fills it. You can monitor it in the **Open Orders** panel.
    Cancel it at any time by clicking the cancel icon in that panel.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  For a deeper look at what happens between signing and settlement — including
  how EIP-712 signatures are verified and how trades are written on-chain — see
  [How Perpetra Works](/get-started/how-it-works).
</Tip>

## Closing a position

Closing a position follows the same flow as opening one, but in reverse. Find your open position in the **Positions** panel, then click **Close**. You can close fully or enter a partial size to close only part of your exposure.

The close is submitted as a signed order, exactly like an open. Under the hood, Perpetra applies a **reduce-only** flag automatically, which means the close order can never accidentally overshoot and flip you into a new position on the opposite side. If the order size exactly matches your open position, you close to flat. See [Order Types & Reduce-Only](/trading/order-types-and-reduce-only) for more on how reduce-only orders work.
