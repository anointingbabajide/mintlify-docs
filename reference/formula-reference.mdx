Every formula Perpetra uses to validate, price, and settle your trades is listed here in one place. All prices and USD amounts are 18-decimal fixed-point values unless stated otherwise. All `Bps` values are expressed out of `10,000` — for example, `50 bps = 0.5%`. Use this page as a quick reference when you want to understand exactly how a number on the trading interface is calculated.

---

## 1. Pre-Trade Checks

These checks run inside **RiskEngine** before any position is opened or increased. Every check must pass or the transaction reverts.

### Leverage Bounds

```text
leverage = sizeUsd / collateralAmount

minLeverage <= leverage <= maxLeverage
```

Perpetra evaluates both bounds without division to avoid precision loss:

```text
sizeUsd >= collateralAmount * minLeverage
collateralAmount * maxLeverage >= sizeUsd
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sizeUsd` | Notional value of the position you want to open, in USD (18 dec) |
| `collateralAmount` | USDC you are depositing as margin for this position (18 dec) |
| `minLeverage` | Protocol minimum leverage (e.g. 1×) |
| `maxLeverage` | Protocol maximum leverage (e.g. 50×) |

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Initial Margin

```text
initialMarginRequired = sizeUsd * initialMarginBps / 10_000
collateralAmount >= initialMarginRequired
```

This check is independent of the leverage bounds above — both must pass.

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `initialMarginBps` | Per-market initial margin requirement in basis points |
| `initialMarginRequired` | Minimum collateral needed to open the position |

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Open Interest Cap

Tracked separately per side (long and short):

```text
newOI = currentOI + sizeUsd        // for a new position
newOI = currentOI + sizeDelta      // for an increase on an existing position

newOI <= maxLongOpenInterest       // checked for longs
newOI <= maxShortOpenInterest      // checked for shorts
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `currentOI` | Total USD notional currently open on this side of the market |
| `sizeUsd` / `sizeDelta` | New notional being added |
| `maxLongOpenInterest` | Protocol cap on total long exposure per market |
| `maxShortOpenInterest` | Protocol cap on total short exposure per market |

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

## 2. PnL, Margin Ratio & Liquidation

### Unrealized PnL

```text
currentValue = sizeUsd * markPrice / entryPrice

Long:  unrealizedPnl = currentValue - sizeUsd
Short: unrealizedPnl = sizeUsd - currentValue
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sizeUsd` | Notional value of your position at entry (18 dec) |
| `markPrice` | Current mark price used for PnL (18 dec) |
| `entryPrice` | Price at which the position was originally opened (18 dec) |
| `currentValue` | Present value of your position at the current mark price |

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Effective Collateral

```text
effectiveCollateral = max(0, collateralAmount + unrealizedPnl)
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `collateralAmount` | USDC deposited as margin for this position |
| `unrealizedPnl` | Current paper profit/loss (can be negative) |

Effective collateral floors at zero — your loss can never exceed your deposited collateral in isolated mode.

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Margin Ratio

```text
marginRatioBps = effectiveCollateral * 10_000 / sizeUsd
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `effectiveCollateral` | Collateral after accounting for unrealized PnL |
| `sizeUsd` | Notional position size |
| `marginRatioBps` | Resulting ratio in basis points (out of 10,000) |

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Maintenance Margin

```text
maintenanceMargin = sizeUsd * maintenanceMarginBps / 10_000
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `maintenanceMarginBps` | Per-market maintenance margin rate in basis points |
| `maintenanceMargin` | Minimum collateral required to keep the position alive |

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Liquidation Trigger

```text
isLiquidatable = effectiveCollateral < maintenanceMargin
```

When this condition is true, a keeper can submit a liquidation transaction for your position.

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Liquidation Price (Isolated)

The mark price at which your isolated position hits the liquidation trigger:

```text
Long:  liqPrice = entryPrice * (sizeUsd - collateralAmount + maintenanceMargin) / sizeUsd
Short: liqPrice = entryPrice * (sizeUsd + collateralAmount - maintenanceMargin) / sizeUsd
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `entryPrice` | Price at which the position was opened |
| `sizeUsd` | Notional position size |
| `collateralAmount` | Deposited isolated margin |
| `maintenanceMargin` | Minimum collateral required (derived from `maintenanceMarginBps`) |

<Note>
  Adding more collateral to an isolated position raises its liquidation price buffer. Withdrawing collateral moves the liquidation price closer to the current mark price.
</Note>

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

## 3. Cross-Margin Liquidation

### Account Equity Check

For cross-margin, Perpetra evaluates all your open positions in that collateral token together:

```text
accountEquity = max(0, vaultBalance + sum(unrealizedPnl across all cross positions))
totalMaintenanceRequired = sum(sizeUsd_i * maintenanceMarginBps_i / 10_000)

isLiquidatable = accountEquity < totalMaintenanceRequired
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `vaultBalance` | Your total deposited USDC balance in the Vault |
| `unrealizedPnl` | Paper PnL on each individual cross position (can be negative) |
| `sizeUsd_i` | Notional size of the *i*-th cross position |
| `maintenanceMarginBps_i` | Maintenance margin rate for the *i*-th position's market |

<Warning>
  The caller-supplied position set must exactly match your actual open cross-position count for that token. Omitting even a single healthy position causes the check to revert, preventing any manipulation of the result.
</Warning>

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

---

### Cross Liquidation Price (Per Position)

This is a snapshot estimate that holds all other positions' PnL fixed at their current mark price:

```text
requiredPnl = (otherMaintenance + targetMaintenance) - vaultBalance - otherPnl

Long:  liqPrice = entryPrice * (requiredPnl + targetSizeUsd) / targetSizeUsd
Short: liqPrice = entryPrice * (targetSizeUsd - requiredPnl) / targetSizeUsd
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `otherMaintenance` | Sum of maintenance margins for all cross positions except the target |
| `targetMaintenance` | Maintenance margin of the position being estimated |
| `vaultBalance` | Your current USDC balance in the Vault |
| `otherPnl` | Sum of unrealized PnL on every cross position except the target |
| `targetSizeUsd` | Notional size of the position being estimated |
| `entryPrice` | Entry price of the position being estimated |

<Info>
  This number is a point-in-time estimate only. It becomes stale the moment any other position in your cross book moves. It is not a standing guarantee.
</Info>

**Contract:** `RiskEngine`

## 4. Funding

Funding is accrued continuously and settled when you open, modify, or close a position.

### Premium and Funding Rate

```text
premium = (markPrice - indexPrice) / indexPrice
fundingRate = clamp(premium, -maxFundingRateBps, +maxFundingRateBps)
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `markPrice` | Smoothed reference price used for PnL |
| `indexPrice` | Raw oracle price used as the spot reference |
| `premium` | Percentage divergence of mark from index |
| `maxFundingRateBps` | Per-interval funding rate cap in basis points |
| `fundingRate` | Actual rate applied this interval (clamped) |

**Contract:** `FundingManager`

---

### Cumulative Funding Index

```text
indexDelta = fundingRate * timeDelta / fundingInterval
cumulativeIndex += indexDelta
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `timeDelta` | Seconds elapsed since the last funding update |
| `fundingInterval` | Configured funding period length in seconds |
| `indexDelta` | Incremental change to the cumulative index this update |
| `cumulativeIndex` | Running total used to calculate each position's funding owed |

**Contract:** `FundingManager`

---

### Funding Owed by a Position

```text
rawFunding = sizeUsd * (currentIndex - positionFundingIndex) / RATE_PRECISION

Long:  fundingOwed = rawFunding
Short: fundingOwed = -rawFunding
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sizeUsd` | Notional size of the position |
| `currentIndex` | The latest cumulative funding index |
| `positionFundingIndex` | The cumulative index recorded when the position was last updated |
| `RATE_PRECISION` | Fixed-point precision constant for the rate calculation |
| `fundingOwed` | Amount owed (positive = position pays, negative = position receives) |

A positive `fundingOwed` means your position owes funding to the other side. A negative value means you receive funding.

**Contract:** `FundingManager`

## 5. Fees

### Trading Fee

```text
fee = sizeUsd * feeBps / 10_000
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sizeUsd` | Notional value of the trade |
| `feeBps` | `takerFeeBps` for market orders; `makerFeeBps` for limit orders |
| `fee` | Amount charged in USDC |

**Contract:** `FeeCollector`

---

### Liquidation Fee

```text
fee = sizeUsd * liquidationFeeBps / 10_000
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sizeUsd` | Notional value of the liquidated position |
| `liquidationFeeBps` | Liquidation penalty rate in basis points |

**Contract:** `FeeCollector`

---

### Fee Distribution Split

```text
insuranceAmount = pendingFees * insuranceFundBps / 10_000
treasuryAmount  = pendingFees - insuranceAmount
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pendingFees` | Total fees accumulated and ready for distribution |
| `insuranceFundBps` | Share directed to the Insurance Fund in basis points |
| `insuranceAmount` | Portion sent to the Insurance Fund |
| `treasuryAmount` | Remainder sent to the protocol treasury |

**Contract:** `FeeCollector`

---

## 6. Liquidation Settlement

When a position is liquidated, effective collateral is calculated first, then split against the keeper fee:

```text
effectiveCollateral = collateralAmount + unrealizedPnl - fundingOwed
```

That value is then split according to three cases:

```text
Case 1 — effectiveCollateral >= keeperFee:
    keeper receives keeperFee
    remainder is returned to the trader

Case 2 — 0 < effectiveCollateral < keeperFee:
    keeper receives effectiveCollateral
    Insurance Fund covers the shortfall (keeperFee - effectiveCollateral)

Case 3 — effectiveCollateral <= 0:
    keeper receives 0
    Insurance Fund covers the full keeperFee
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `collateralAmount` | Margin deposited for the position |
| `unrealizedPnl` | Paper PnL at the time of liquidation |
| `fundingOwed` | Accrued funding cost deducted from collateral |
| `keeperFee` | Fixed reward paid to the keeper bot that triggered the liquidation |

**Contract:** `Liquidator`

## 7. Slippage

Slippage is enforced off-chain by the Matcher before a trade is routed to settlement:

```text
diffBps = |oraclePrice - limitPrice| * 10_000 / limitPrice

reject if diffBps > maxSlippageBps
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `oraclePrice` | Current oracle price at the time of matching |
| `limitPrice` | The price embedded in your signed order |
| `diffBps` | Deviation between oracle price and your limit price, in basis points |
| `maxSlippageBps` | Maximum tolerated deviation; orders breaching this threshold are rejected |

**Contract:** Matcher (off-chain component)

---

## 8. Points

Points accrue on both sides of a trade — opening and closing are each counted separately.

### Trade Points

```text
tradePointsEarned = volumeUSD * tradePointsPerUSD / 1e6
```

### Deposit Points

```text
depositPointsEarned = amountUSD * depositPointsPerUSD / 1e6
```

### Referrer Points

```text
referrerPointsEarned = baseEarned * referralBps / 10_000
```

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `volumeUSD` | Notional volume of the trade in USD (18 dec) |
| `tradePointsPerUSD` | Protocol rate: points awarded per USD of volume (scaled to 1e6) |
| `amountUSD` | Amount deposited into the Vault in USD |
| `depositPointsPerUSD` | Protocol rate: points awarded per USD deposited (scaled to 1e6) |
| `baseEarned` | Points earned by the referee on a given action |
| `referralBps` | Share of the referee's points credited to the referrer, in basis points |

**Contract:** `PointsSystem`
